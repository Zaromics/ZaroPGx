Methodology

The sample's genetic data were analyzed with the ZaroPGx pipeline, culminating in PharmCAT (Pharmacogenomics Clinical Annotation Tool), which references clinical guidelines published by CPIC (Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium), DPWG (Dutch Pharmacogenetics Working Group) and the FDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration). Genetic variants were processed to identify star alleles and diplotypes, which were then mapped to phenotypes and matched with relevant recommendations.
